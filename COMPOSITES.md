# odml composite coverage program

Goal: adopt and benchmark each odml composite / custom op across these
examples, one at a time, and report what we find - a running showcase of the
composite path from an external C++ Tensor API user. Started with sdpa and
runtime_bmm; we continue down the list.

| composite | status here | measured / notes |
|---|---|---|
| odml.runtime_bmm | ADOPTED (talker `--attention=rbmm`) | attention 3.0-3.4x vs raw ops on Metal fp32, exact at every fill; e2e frame -4-5%; decompositions also the fastest CPU form. CL init issue open (ledger #6) |
| odml.scaled_dot_product_attention | ADOPTED for the diffusion step (`--attention=sdpa` in diffusion_main); decode shapes stay rbmm | Decode (q_seq=1, talker): Metal kernel slower than the raw BMM chain (4.4 vs 2.6 ms/28 blocks) - rbmm stays the decode choice. Full-sequence (diffusion, CLEAN-MACHINE NUMBERS 7/23 - the 7/22 figures were inflated ~2x by host contention and mislabeled fp32 while running at the fp16 delegate default): at the LLaDA shape (MHA 32/32, 4096-dim, 4L probe) sdpa is exact on Metal (real-weight probe: GPU ids == CPU ids at every position, at BOTH explicit fp32 and fp16) and -3.8%/step at S=1024 fp32 (154.1 -> 148.2 ms), -2.5% fp16 (134.8 -> 131.5); S=256 neutral; at the 1024-dim GQA 28L shape the folded form is exact and NEUTRAL on a quiet machine (27.45 vs 27.25-27.48 fp32) - the earlier "faster at 28L" was contention amplifying dispatch overhead. Node-count effect on the full 32L graph: 1816 raw ops -> 1402 (sdpa) -> 1077 with rms too (-41%). Correctness boundaries unchanged, ledger #13/#14: Metal CLAIMS un-folded grouped K/V at q_seq>1 but computes wrong results (q_seq=1 rejects cleanly), and the CPU runtime substitutes its own sdpa lowering for the authored decomposition, which breaks for batched presentations - CPU-parity runs must use `--attention=raw` (or `sdpa_folddirect`) |
| odml.cache_update | BENCHED single-step + multi-step harness (attn_bench `--variant=cache_rbmm [--multi_steps=N --feedback_loops]`) | WORKS on Metal within one Run(): delegate matches the composite and pairs with runtime_bmm; single decode step ~0.35 ms, exact vs CPU at fill=1. Contract learned: caches are delegate-managed on GPU (K stored in the QK FC-weights layout, V in the AV layout; the host-side cache inputs feed only the CPU decomposition), and the control tensor packs write offset (elem 0), active tokens (elem 1) and the runtime_bmm bound (elem 2) in one [1,1,1,7] int32. Multi-step (7/22): WITHOUT feedback loops the Metal delegate cache resets every Run() (identical mismatches whether host caches carry zeros or real prefixes - they are never read); WITH PR #8796 FeedbackLoopConfig (cache_k/v_in <-> cache_k/v_new), 64 chained decode steps are bit-exact on CPU, and Metal fails the second Run() at buffer re-registration (ledger #15). Talker adoption waits on that gap |
| odml.rms_norm | ADOPTED as an option (`--norms=composite` in talker_main and diffusion_main) | talker 28L Metal: fp32 29.0 -> 28.1 ms/frame, fp16 21.3 -> 20.3 (about -1 ms on top of runtime_bmm; combined stack -7.5 to -8.6% vs the original raw graph). diffusion RE-CORRECTED 7/23 (clean machine; the 7/22 directional claims both ways were host-contention artifacts, and a flag-wiring bug meant the original "neutral" was raw-vs-raw): at the 1024-dim GQA 28L shape rms is neutral at fp32 and ~-3.5% at fp16 (25.40 -> 24.46-24.50 ms/step); at the LLaDA 4096-dim shape seq1024 it is neutral at fp32 (154.1 raw vs 153.9-154.6 rms) and does not add on top of sdpa (stack 148.0-150.5 ~= sdpa alone 148.2). CPU decompositions bit-identical. Note: the fused kernel's reduction order differs from the decomposition, so fp32 GPU greedy trajectories shift - use --norms=raw for CPU-parity verification runs |
| odml.group_norm / layer_norm | N/A here | no group-norm in these models |
| moe (custom) | FUTURE | delegate has a MoE parser; connects to the generative-API MoE lane |
| custom_call.rotary_positional_embedding | EVALUATE | our rope rows are host-precomputed inputs by design (one serialized graph serves any position); worth an A/B against the custom op |
